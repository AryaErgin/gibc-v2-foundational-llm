# Data Sources

FineWeb source: `HuggingFaceFW/fineweb`, official `sample-10BT` config, pinned immutable revision `9bb295ddab0e05d785b879661af7260fed5140fc`, field `text`, ODC-BY license. SHA-256 content IDs and `sha256(seed:id) % 10000 < 200` give deterministic 2% validation/98% training assignment (seed 42).

Tokenizer training uses only accepted train documents (about 100 MiB), byte-level BPE from scratch, exactly 8192 entries, and the one special token `<|endoftext|>`. EXP-001B will record a newly regenerated tokenizer SHA-256 in its fresh bounded artifact; EXP-001A's hash `3adc8a1d02f9e9d28d08eb8761206732877eb25c65aab2f80f34d1b6c1a1f122` remains historical evidence and is not reused as EXP-001B provenance.

Decontamination normalizes NFKC/casefold/word-punctuation tokens, hashes normalized 13-grams, and rejects overlap against a local-only index. Immutable public benchmark revisions and requested splits are committed in `provenance/exp001-benchmark-revisions.json`: HellaSwag train/validation/test; ARC-Easy train/validation/test; PIQA train/dev/test; WinoGrande train/validation/test; WikiText-103 validation/test only. Hidden labels are never required, and diagnostics contain hashes/counts/provenance rather than benchmark text. EXP-001A's 34,302 scanned / 34 rejected values were measured against its narrower index and remain historical, not EXP-001B counts. No overlap detected by this procedure does not prove zero contamination.

For the eventual baseline, the full local representation is one non-cycled `uint16` stream of exactly 100,007,937 stored tokens. It yields 195,328 successive 513-token views, therefore 3,052 optimizer updates of 64 sequences and exactly 100,007,936 prediction tokens. Requested views convert only their 513 IDs to `torch.long` before embedding lookup; duplicated full-corpus input/target tensors are not materialized.
