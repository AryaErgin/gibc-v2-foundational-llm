# EXP-012 — Fresh 2.4B-token long-horizon calibration

## Hypothesis

For frozen Near-Cap Recipe v3, a fresh 73,242-step cosine schedule from seed-42 initialization will retain material validation improvement through a 2.4B-class horizon. This is a single horizon decision, not a sequence of horizon probes.

## Frozen controls

- Recipe v3: 8,192 vocabulary; width 640; 9 layers; 20 heads × 32; SwiGLU/SiLU gate with `d_ff=1728`; tied embeddings/output; exactly 49,860,480 trainable parameters.
- Data Recipe v1, deterministic target 2:1 FineWeb/FineWeb-Edu mixture; frozen tokenizer, held-out General/Edu validations, source revisions, global canonical-content SHA-256 deduplication, and 13-gram benchmark contamination index semantics.
- Context 512; seed 42; BF16 autocast with FP32 parameters/optimizer state; AdamW β=(0.9,0.95), eps=1e-8, matrix decay=0.1; clip 1.0; physical 32 × accumulation 2; exactly 32,768 prediction tokens/update.
- Native Windows, OMEN Performance mode, and AC power are recorded systems controls.

## Authorized horizon

- Fresh initialization only; no EXP-011 checkpoint/model/schedule reuse.
- 73,242 updates; 2,399,993,856 prediction tokens; 2,399,993,857 stored uint16 token IDs.
- Cosine peak/min LR 6e-4/6e-5 with 100-step warmup and total schedule horizon 73,242 from step zero.
- Milestones: steps 0, 9,156, 18,312, 27,468, 36,624, 45,780, 54,936, 64,092, and terminal 73,242. The terminal tranche is 9,150 updates (299,827,200 prediction tokens).

## Data gates

The existing builder has no serializable global-dedup mixer state. EXP-012 therefore deterministically rebuilds from stream zero, using one global dedup state across the entire 2.4B artifact. The build is authorized only if it records and independently verifies exact byte/token prefixes for EXP-004 (300M), EXP-006 (900M), and the verified EXP-011 1.5B artifact, plus stream/manifest/tokenizer hashes, pinned source provenance, target and realized source contributions, contamination-index reuse, and screening evidence.

## Preflight and stop conditions

Before full training: unit/invariant tests, parameter recount, strict artifact loader, a finite 60-update preflight, and a fresh-process step-60 → step-61 resume test. Stop on a hash/prefix/provenance/parameter/control mismatch, non-finite loss or gradient, or checkpoint/resume failure.

## Terminal diagnostic

For the final approximately 300M-token tranche, combined validation gain (previous combined loss minus terminal combined loss) is classified as: ≥0.05 strongly data-limited; 0.025–<0.05 meaningfully data-limited; <0.025 approaching diminishing returns. This diagnostic does not authorize further training. EXP-012 stops for research review at step 73,242. Official benchmarks are not authorized.
