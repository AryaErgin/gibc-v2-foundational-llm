# EXP-010 — Near-Cap SwiGLU Depth/Width Allocation

## Hypothesis

At the frozen approximately 50M SwiGLU scale, a moderately deeper/narrower allocation improves final combined frozen general/Edu validation loss enough to replace Near-Cap Architecture Recipe v3. EXP-007 compared a related allocation only under GELU; EXP-008 changed the MLP to SwiGLU while retaining the v2 allocation. This experiment isolates that untested interaction.

## Control

Frozen Recipe v3 / EXP-008A is the existing control and is not retrained: vocabulary 8,192; d_model 640; 9 layers; 20 heads x 32 head dimension; SiLU-gated SwiGLU d_ff 1,728; tied embeddings/output; and exactly 49,860,480 trainable parameters. Its final general/Edu/combined losses are `3.5561715066 / 3.2465001345 / 3.4013358206`.

## Candidate EXP-010A

Candidate EXP-010A changes only the **depth/width parameter allocation** within the same SwiGLU architecture family: d_model 608; 10 layers; 19 heads x 32 head dimension; SiLU-gated SwiGLU d_ff 1,656. It retains vocabulary 8,192, tied embeddings/output, RMSNorm, RoPE, standard causal MHA, no bias/dropout, context 512, and the same initialization policy. Its exact independently instantiated trainable parameter count is **49,985,504**, which is below the 50,000,000 cap.

The allocation is the sole principal scientific variable. Layer count, width, head count, and d_ff are coupled coordinates of that allocation; no optimizer, learning-rate, data, or systems change is permitted.

## Frozen invariants

EXP-010A must retain the exact first 300,023,808-prediction-token non-cycled EXP-004 stream, SHA-256 `8e727fa2a2614751a1c34d7f9ac411dfebeb379a09f584ba4f7f418d1059cea1`; tokenizer SHA-256 `c5592fba176c3d2f7915a3812559a24d7a669206f4a22484b053c8a9ce08be14`; frozen general/Edu validation tensors and hashes; data order; context 512; seed 42; BF16 autocast with FP32 parameters/optimizer state; AdamW betas 0.9/0.95 and eps 1e-8; matrix decay 0.1; clip 1.0; peak/min LR 6e-4/6e-5; 100-update warmup; 9,156-update cosine horizon; physical batch 32 x 2 accumulation; 32,768 effective prediction tokens/update; and validation/checkpoint cadence 3,052 / 6,104 / 9,156. Native Windows, OMEN Performance mode, and AC power remain frozen systems controls.

## Preflight

Before any full run, instantiate and count the candidate; verify all stream/tokenizer/validation hashes; run unit/invariant tests; then, only with separate research authorization, run a 60-update finite-loss preflight at the fixed 32 x 2 path and a fresh-process one-update resume from its step-60 checkpoint. Preflight loss is engineering readiness evidence only and must not select an architecture.

## Predeclared full-horizon decision

Primary metric: `combined = (general + Edu) / 2` after exactly 9,156 updates / 300,023,808 prediction tokens.

- Candidate advantage at least `0.02` nats: EXP-010A is the capability winner and replaces Recipe v3.
- Absolute combined difference below `0.02` nats: engineering tie; retain Recipe v3 unless the candidate is at least as efficient in throughput and peak allocated memory and materially simpler to implement. No threshold may be changed after results are known.
- Candidate disadvantage at least `0.02` nats: reject EXP-010A and retain Recipe v3.
- Split guard: if the candidate improves combined by at least `0.02` nats but worsens either general or Edu loss by more than `0.02` nats, do not automatically promote it; flag research review.

## Expected cost and abort conditions

One authorized full candidate would require exactly 300,023,808 prediction tokens, 9,156 updates, and approximately `8.9981047529275392e16` FLOPs (`6 x parameters x tokens`, about 0.08998 EFLOPs). Based on the Recipe v3 control, expected runtime is roughly 3,100 seconds plus checkpoint/validation overhead; this is an estimate, not a result.

Abort immediately and preserve evidence if: the instantiated count exceeds 50,000,000; any frozen hash or manifest invariant differs; the fixed 32 x 2 physical batch cannot run; non-finite loss/gradient occurs; checkpoint/resume fails to preserve the exact cursor; a required validation/checkpoint is missing; or the process/environment differs from the frozen native Windows/Performance/AC control. Do not substitute a smaller model, batch, context, token budget, or alternate backend.

## Authorized preflight result

Subsequent research authorization permitted the bounded preflight and full candidate run. The 60-update preflight at source commit `430d3757ff9f34d43e4bc28e347e42b60fc46853` passed with finite train/general/Edu losses, exact 49,985,504 parameters, the fixed 32 x 2 physical batch, and mean throughput `96,890.71` tok/s. Its peak allocated/reserved memory was `8,061,879,808 / 8,835,301,376` bytes. A fresh process restored the step-60 checkpoint and completed step 61 at `next_sequence_index=3904` with finite losses. Exact evidence is in `results/EXP-010-preflight.md`.
