# EXP-006 — Training-Horizon Scaling Preparation

## Question

For the frozen EXP-005B Architecture Recipe v1 and EXP-004 Data Recipe v1, how much additional internal language-modeling benefit remains when the horizon increases from 300,023,808 to 900,071,424 unique prediction tokens with a horizon-matched cosine schedule?

This is a training-horizon plus horizon-matched-schedule intervention. It does not establish pure token-count causality. EXP-006 step 9,156 is not a controlled reproduction of EXP-005B's step 9,156 because the cosine horizons differ (27,468 versus 9,156).

## Frozen controls

The model is exactly EXP-005B: vocab 8,192; d_model 384; 10 layers; 12 heads; head_dim/rotary_dim 32; d_ff 1,536; exact GELU; pre-RMSNorm eps 1e-5; standard causal MHA; RoPE theta 10,000; tied embeddings/output; no linear bias; dropout zero; init std 0.02. Its exact real-model count is 20,848,512.

The training recipe remains seed 42, BF16 autocast with FP32 parameters and optimizer state, AdamW betas 0.9/0.95/eps 1e-8, matrix weight decay 0.1, clip 1.0, peak/min LR 6e-4/6e-5, 100 warmup updates, effective batch 32,768 prediction tokens, and physical batch 32 sequences x 2 accumulation. EXP-006 changes only the full horizon to 27,468 updates / 900,071,424 prediction tokens, with cosine decay defined across all 27,468 updates.

## Data and prefix control

The stream is a deterministic, non-cycled 2:1 target mixture of 600,047,616 FineWeb and 300,023,808 FineWeb-Edu prediction tokens. It reuses the exact pinned repositories/revisions, frozen tokenizer SHA `c5592fba176c3d2f7915a3812559a24d7a669206f4a22484b053c8a9ce08be14`, content-hash split, normalized 13-gram contamination screen, EOD insertion, whole-document token-deficit mixing, and cross-source canonical-content SHA-256 deduplication.

Materialization rebuilds the mixed stream from the beginning. Before it writes a usable manifest, it hashes exactly the first 600,047,618 raw bytes (300,023,809 uint16 IDs) and requires EXP-004's stream SHA `8e727fa2a2614751a1c34d7f9ac411dfebeb379a09f584ba4f7f418d1059cea1`. A mismatch hard-fails and leaves no training-authorized EXP-006 manifest.

The general and educational validation tensors are frozen exactly at their established input/target hashes. Dual validation and resumable checkpoints are predeclared for steps 0, 9,156, 18,312, and 27,468. The primary metric is the equal-token mean of general and educational validation loss.

## Predeclared final-tranche interpretation

From 600M to 900M tokens: a combined-loss decrease of at least 0.05 nats indicates strong data limitation; 0.02–0.05 is useful but diminishing; below 0.02 indicates approaching saturation at this scale. These are project engineering thresholds, not claims of statistical significance.

## Result and authorization boundary

The approved full horizon completed at training source commit `3d3fd727adb17ebda52be7c06f51f0123b3dcff2`: 27,468 updates and 900,071,424 prediction tokens. The final combined loss was `3.3311669528484344`; its 600M-to-900M improvement was `0.0848376601934433`, above the predeclared 0.05-nat strongly-data-limited threshold. Exact immutable evidence is in `results/EXP-006-summary.md`.
