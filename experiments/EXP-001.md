# EXP-001

## Question

Can the approved control architecture be trained reproducibly from scratch with auditable data provenance and correct evaluation infrastructure?

## Status

Infrastructure validated. Controlled values are in `configs/exp001.yaml`; eventual budget is 100M prediction tokens and EXP-001A used exactly 1,966,080. Tests cover count, tying, causality, target shift, shapes, RoPE, determinism, decontamination, overfit, checkpoints, RNG restore, and resume. Limited zero-shot lm-eval calls are integration-only, not official scores. Full training remains unauthorized pending research review.
