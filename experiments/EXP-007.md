# EXP-007 — Near-Cap Architecture Comparison Preparation

## Question and frozen controls

At approximately matched 49.4M total parameters, EXP-007 compares two allocations under the exact frozen EXP-004 Data Recipe v1 stream, tokenizer, dual validations, seed, initialization, context, optimizer, schedule, and physical batch. Both use 9,156 updates / 300,023,808 prediction tokens, a 100-step warmup, and cosine horizon 9,156. No stream is copied, rematerialized, reordered, or cycled.

EXP-007A is d_model 608, 10 layers, 19 heads of head_dim 32, d_ff 2432; its exact required count is 49,353,184. EXP-007B is d_model 640, 9 layers, 20 heads of head_dim 32, d_ff 2560; its exact required count is 49,491,840. The 138,656-parameter difference is approximately 0.281%. Both remain below the 50M competition cap.

The runner references `artifacts/exp004-full/train-token-stream.uint16` directly and requires SHA-256 `8e727fa2a2614751a1c34d7f9ac411dfebeb379a09f584ba4f7f418d1059cea1`, plus the established frozen tokenizer and general/educational validation hashes. The physical batch is initially fixed at 32 sequences x 2 accumulation. If it does not fit, that is an engineering finding; it must not be silently changed.

## Preflight and decision rule

Equal 60-update preflights verify construction, finite loss/gradients, BF16 operation, checkpoint/restart cursor continuity, throughput, and allocated/reserved VRAM. They are not architecture results and their losses must not select a winner.

The eventual primary metric is final equal-token `combined_validation_loss = (general_validation_loss + edu_validation_loss) / 2`. A final difference of at least 0.02 nats selects the lower-loss candidate; below 0.02 is an engineering tie decided by efficiency, memory, and scaling behavior. No official benchmark is part of EXP-007.

## Completion and decision

Both equal 60-update engineering preflights passed at the required initial 32 sequences x 2 accumulation, including fresh-process one-update resume. The complete 9,156-update / 300,023,808-token runs subsequently finished on implementation commit `9523e744082fb4dedbcd9856964cb625bde08b0c`; exact evidence is in `results/EXP-007-summary.md`.

EXP-007A's final combined validation loss was numerically lower: `3.4257752001` versus EXP-007B's `3.4314021170`. The difference B-A is `+0.0056269169` nats, strictly below the predeclared `0.02` engineering-tie threshold. This is therefore an engineering tie, not a lower-loss architecture selection. Under the predeclared efficiency tiebreak, EXP-007B is selected for higher mean throughput and lower peak allocated memory.

Near-Cap Architecture Recipe v2 is frozen as EXP-007B: vocabulary 8,192; d_model 640; 9 layers; 20 heads of head dimension 32; d_ff 2,560; exact parameter count 49,491,840. No official benchmark was run for either candidate.
