# Reviewer risks and evidence gaps

- Single-machine/runtime evidence may not generalize across hardware or kernels.
- Negative LLR and Magma results are constrained transfer findings, not
  universal optimizer conclusions.
- Internal validation and official benchmark evidence have distinct roles.
- WSD is an established scheduling approach; novelty must not be claimed.
- EXP-017A horizon evidence is currently pending because Attempt 1 was a
  thermal technical abort without a scientific result.
- The physical cooling setup (AC, hard surface, unobstructed vents) requires
  user confirmation; no unattended launch is allowed without trusted CPU
  package telemetry.
