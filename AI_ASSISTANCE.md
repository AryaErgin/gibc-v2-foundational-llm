# AI assistance disclosure

AI assistance was material throughout the project. It supported the development process; **it is not the submitted model**. EXP-020 is not a ChatGPT/Codex/API wrapper. Its neural weights and tokenizer were trained from scratch.

| Area | Assistance disclosed |
|---|---|
| Research and explanation/learning | ChatGPT technical explanations and literature interpretation; Deep Research discovery/synthesis |
| Experiment planning | Drafting/reviewing hypotheses, controlled comparisons, preregistrations, promotion gates and operational plans |
| Code drafting/review/debugging | Codex implementation, review, tests, profiling, runtime/thermal diagnosis, data-builder optimization and authorized supervision |
| Analysis | Extracting recorded metrics, checking hashes/provenance, interpretation support and adversarial review |
| Documentation and paper support | Drafting/editing reports, claims/evidence tables, related-work organization and manuscript outlines |
| Submission packaging | README/Devpost prose, recorded-data figure scripts/captions, video storyboard/narration, freshness audit, hostile-judge review, public-release hash verification and clean-environment inference smoke |

This does not imply every AI suggestion was adopted. Code, configuration, tests and frozen artifacts—not AI assertions—support the scientific claims.

ChatGPT assisted with research synthesis, experiment specification review, explanations, interpretation and adversarial questions. Deep Research assisted literature discovery and synthesis. OpenAI Codex assisted implementation, tests, profiling, debugging, artifact/provenance audits and submission documentation, including the figure-generation scripts and draft demo narration.

Human research review retained authority over scientific questions, frozen controls, run authorization, promotion/rejection gates, checkpoint selection, benchmark execution and publication. AI output was checked against configuration, code, experiment records, hashes and measured artifacts; it is not an independent source of experimental truth.

The repository records from-scratch model and tokenizer training, without pretrained model initialization, fine-tuning, distillation or AI-generated training examples. AI assistance did not supply benchmark answers for training. Public benchmark data was used for exclusion screening and separately authorized evaluation; the final model was selected before its official scoring.

The submission's innovation claim concerns the project's controlled experiments and reproducible engineering, not authorship of Transformer, RoPE, RMSNorm, SwiGLU, AdamW or the benchmark harness. External methods and software are credited in [SOURCE_LEDGER.md](SOURCE_LEDGER.md), [CODE_ATTRIBUTION.md](CODE_ATTRIBUTION.md) and [REFERENCES.bib](REFERENCES.bib).

The final presentation draft and any recorded video remain subject to human review. No model, tokenizer, training data or official result was changed during submission packaging.
